What this means

Hidden achievements can’t count as active

A character is active when their latest public achievement is from the last {{$.ActivityWindow.Long}}. Someone who hides their achievements can’t count as active, however often they play, so every active-player figure is a lower bound.

Story progress is a lower bound too

Expansion completions come from story achievements. A character who hides them never shows as finishing an expansion, so the real completion rates are higher.

Not yet checked isn’t a choice

These characters are waiting for the census to read their achievements for the first time. Once checked, each one moves to public or private.

Everything else still counts

Name, world, race, Grand Company and job levels come from the public profile, so these characters still count in population, races, Grand Companies and jobs.
